# 📊 Why People Quit: A Deep Dive into Subscription Churn

## 🎯 What is this project about?
I wanted to look under the hood of subscription services to see exactly why people decide to cancel their accounts. Using data from 1,000 subscribers, I mapped out the biggest pain points that cause users to drop out and figured out how businesses can stop it before it happens.

## 💡 What the data told me
* **Monthly plans are risky**: People paying month-to-month are **3 times more likely to cancel** compared to those locked into an annual plan.
* **Customer support is a breaking point**: If a user has to open more than **2 tech support tickets**, their chances of quitting jump by 80%. They simply lose patience.
* **The 6-month sweet spot**: If a customer sticks around past the 6-month mark, their lifetime value stabilizes, and they rarely leave.

## 🛠️ Tools I used
* **Python** for organizing and cleaning the dataset.
* **Pandas & NumPy** to calculate customer lifetime values.
* **Seaborn & Matplotlib** to build the visual charts.

## 🚀 How a business can fix this
1. **Fix support issues fast**: Flag users immediately when they open their second support ticket so a real manager can help them before they get frustrated and quit.
2. **Push the annual upgrade early**: Offer a small discount to monthly users right around their 3rd month to shift them onto a steadier, long-term plan.
